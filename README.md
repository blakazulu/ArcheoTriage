# ArcheoTriage

**First Aid for Archaeologists** | עזרה ראשונה לארכיאולוג

## What is ArcheoTriage?

ArcheoTriage is a mobile-first app designed to help archaeologists and students protect artifacts at the most critical moment - the moment of discovery in the field.

The name combines "Archaeology" + "Triage" - reflecting the app's core mission: helping field workers quickly assess and prioritize artifact care, just like medical triage prioritizes patient treatment.

## The Problem

Archaeological artifacts are irreplaceable evidence of human history. Yet they face the greatest risk of damage not in museums or labs, but in the field - the moment they're unearthed after centuries underground.

Sudden exposure to air, sunlight, humidity changes, temperature shifts, and physical handling can cause irreversible damage before professional conservation even begins.

Currently, there's no standardized system for field preservation. Archaeologists must improvise solutions, and different artifact types require completely different care.

## The Solution

ArcheoTriage provides:

- **Field Guide** - Quick-reference preservation instructions for different artifact types (ceramics, metals, organic materials, textiles, scrolls)
- **Risk Assessment** - Color-coded warnings for environmental dangers (humidity, temperature, shock, light)
- **AI Identification** - Photo-based artifact identification to get the right preservation guide instantly
- **Documentation** - Record and catalog findings with photos and notes
- **Education** - Learn the science behind preservation techniques

## Who Is It For?

- Field archaeologists
- Archaeology students
- Museum staff
- Cultural heritage workers
- Anyone working with archaeological discoveries
